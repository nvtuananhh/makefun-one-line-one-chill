## What it does

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network) writes one
sentence of horror to `/output/entry.txt`:

> The photo app made a memory of my late grandmother smiling, and in every picture since, she is standing one step closer.

The line is 120 characters long. The chill lands on the judge's
brief: it is a single sentence that reads as a horror story, it
frightens by dread that outlasts the line rather than by gore, it
is vivid and specific — a photo app memory, a smiling grandmother,
a figure one step closer in every picture — it is original, with
no mirror, no doll, no basement, and the last words land the
blow: she is standing one step closer. The scare is earned by
what the line withholds — why she moves, and what happens when
she finally arrives.

## How to run
